If you see the PCA plot, parental replicates (`Pr_2`, `Pr_3`) and evolved replicates (`Ev_1`, `Ev_2`, `Ev_3`) form tight, distinct clusters while `Pr_1` is really far from those two parental replicates, especially along the axis that has captured the most variance (PC1). Similarly, in the sample-to-sample distance heatmap, the boxes indicating the distance between `Pr_1` and the two other parental replicates are reddish (high Euclidean distance), while the box indicating the distance between `Pr_2` and `Pr_3` and the boxes indicating the distance between the 3 evolved replicates are light bluish (low Euclidean distance). This indicates that `Pr_1` is significantly different from the two other parental replicates (`Pr_2` and `Pr_3`). As a result, further exploratory analyses such as housekeeping gene expression profiles, normalized count distribution profiles, etc. should be carried out in order to confirm it as a technical outlier before proceeding with downstream analysis, especially after getting compounding evidence from some of the RSeQC scripts as well.
